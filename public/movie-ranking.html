Top Movies of all time according to Swedha

My Favourite South Indian (Tamil) Movie List

Kadhalar Dhinam (1999)

Genre:

Romantic/ Musical

Core Theme:

Classic early- internet cyber- romance through virtual Chatrooms.

Highlights:

Iconic, evergreen soundtrack by A.R. Rahaman and memorable lead pairings

Nanban (2012)

Genre:

Coming-of- Age Comedy- Drama

Core Theme:

Friendship, academin pressure, and pursuing one's true passion.

Highlights:

Directed by S.Shankar, starring T.N. C.M. Thalapathy Vijay; a widely successful and beloved adaptation of 3 idiots.

Comali(2019)

Genre:

Comedy/Drama

Core Theme:

90s Nostalgia vs. Modern Smartphone-addicted generation.

Highlights:

Brilliant Comedic Chemistry between Jayam Ravi and Yogi Babu.
